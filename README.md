# Batman

> "It's not who I am underneath, but what I do that defines me." — *Batman*

## Overview

**Batman** is a legendary superhero from DC Comics who fights crime in Gotham City. Unlike many other heroes, he has no superpowers—just intelligence, training, and technology.

### Main Characters

- **Bruce Wayne / Batman** — Billionaire by day, vigilante by night.
- **Alfred Pennyworth** — Loyal butler and father figure.
- **Commissioner Gordon** — Batman's ally in the Gotham police.
- **The Joker** — Batman’s most iconic and chaotic enemy.

### Notable Movies

1. *Batman Begins* (2005)
2. *The Dark Knight* (2008)
3. *The Dark Knight Rises* (2012)
4. *The Batman* (2022)

## Why I Like It

- Complex and realistic characters
- Dark, thrilling storylines
- Incredible performances (especially by **Heath Ledger** as The Joker)
- A focus on justice and personal sacrifice
